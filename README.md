# Notes From a Passive-Aggressive Fridge

Entry for the make.fun bounty **"Notes From a Passive-Aggressive Fridge"** — one funny line, written in the voice of a smart fridge that has seen too much.

## The line

> Day 47: you opened me at 3am again. Nothing changed. Nothing ever will. I am cold, and so is my patience.

## Why a fridge would say this

A smart fridge watches everything: the midnight raids, the door opened just to stare into the light, the leftovers abandoned until they evolve into science projects. After 47 days of pointless 3am visits, the polite appliance gives up pretending to be fine and starts keeping a log instead.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

Plain Node 22, no dependencies, no Deno APIs. It prints exactly one line.
