# Notes From a Passive-Aggressive Fridge

Entry for the make.fun bounty **"Notes From a Passive-Aggressive Fridge"** — one funny line, written in the voice of a smart fridge that has seen too much.

## The line

> Day 47: you opened me at 3am again. Nothing changed. Nothing ever will. I am cold, and so is my patience.

## The setup

The owner sleepwalks to the kitchen at 3am, opens the door, and stares into the light like it owes him an answer.
He takes nothing, closes the door, and goes back to bed as if nothing happened.
The fridge, meanwhile, records all of it — and after 47 nights, it finally writes back.

## Why a fridge would say this

A smart fridge watches everything: the midnight raids, the door opened just to stare into the light, the leftovers abandoned until they evolve into science projects. After 47 days of pointless 3am visits, the polite appliance gives up pretending to be fine and starts keeping a log instead.

## The voice

The joke lands because the fridge never raises its voice — it just states facts, coldly.
"Nothing changed. Nothing ever will" is the sound of an appliance that has given up on its owner.
And the last line turns the cold into a punchline: the fridge is cold, and so is its patience.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

Plain Node 22, no dependencies, no Deno APIs. It prints exactly one line.
The output is the note itself, nothing more — one line, exactly as the fridge wrote it.
Run it twice and you get the same line twice: the fridge does not improvise.
